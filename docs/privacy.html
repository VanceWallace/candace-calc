Privacy policy and support

CandaceCalc doesn't collect or share any personal information. Your calculations are saved only on your own device.

What the app keeps

To show your paper tape, CandaceCalc saves your calculations and your settings in the app's own storage on your phone. That includes:

  • the numbers and results you print to the tape, with the date and time of each
  • your settings, such as paper position, color, sounds and how long to keep history

This information never leaves your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

How long it's kept

History is deleted automatically after 30, 60 or 90 days, whichever you choose in Settings. You can clear the whole tape at any time with Settings → Clear tape history. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

What the app doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising
  • No data sold or shared with third parties
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone

Children

CandaceCalc doesn't collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts handling data differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
